- Third-party components, references, products and images remain subject to their respective rights and terms.
- Published configurations are **lab-only templates** or **sanitized artifacts supported by evidence**; they never represent actual customer infrastructure.
- The material can be viewed on GitHub in accordance with the platform's terms and applicable law.

**Recommended GitHub settings:** go to **Settings > Features** and disable **Pull requests**, **Issues** and **Discussions** (where enabled). This prevents submissions through those repository features. The repository can remain publicly visible; forking is controlled separately under GitHub's platform rules. Maintenance remains exclusively with the repository owner. To integrate personal branches without pull requests, use a local `git merge` and push the default branch.
